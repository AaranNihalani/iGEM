About the models

How to read these numbers
  • Percentile. The score is compared with 100,000 random 8-mers. 0.95 means only 5% of random windows score higher. This puts every protease on the same scale.
  • Consensus. The harmonic mean of the logistic-regression and PSSM percentiles; high only if both agree.
  • Specificity margin. Target consensus minus the strongest competitor's, within the panel you chose. Near +1: strongly specific. Near 0: a competitor recognises it as well as the target. Negative: a competitor prefers it.
  • Unknown positions. Shown as “-”. They are handled exactly for the additive models and compared with an equally uninformed null.
What these models cannot tell you
  • They see only the eight residues around the bond: no flanking construct, structure, pH, or enzyme concentration.
  • Scores rank resemblance to observed cleavage sites. They are not rate constants or probabilities.
  • Selectivity among MMP-2, -8, -9 and -14 is weak from sequence alone.
  • The LasB model is trained mostly on one protein (elastin) and behaves largely as an elastin-likeness score.
  • Optimised sequences far from natural substrates are extrapolations; test before relying on them.
